See what your electricity really costs, every hour. Pick your Dutch energy supplier and get the price you actually pay on a dynamic contract: the market price plus your supplier's purchase fee, energy tax and VAT.

Shows the price now and next hour, the lowest, highest and average price of today, and the cheapest and most expensive hour still to come. Gas prices too, where your supplier's fee is known.

Use the Flow cards to run your washing machine, dishwasher, heat pump or car charger in the cheapest hours: start when the cheapest hour begins, or only when this hour is one of the cheapest of the day.

You don't need a SolarBuffer. The prices come from the public SolarBuffer price service, see hub.solarbuffer.nl/energieprijzen.
